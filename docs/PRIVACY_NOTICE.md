# Student Monitoring Notice

Your instructor uses the Exam Behavior Monitor browser extension for this examination.

**When it runs:** only after you sign in, select your assigned session, accept this notice and enter the
session code, and only on the registered Google Forms examination tab. It stops when you press End
monitoring or shortly after the scheduled end time.

**What is recorded, with timestamps:** switching away from or returning to the examination tab, opening
another tab (no address or title is recorded), the examination window losing or regaining focus, copy and
paste actions (not the copied content), leaving fullscreen if you were using it, long periods without
mouse or keyboard activity (duration only), and losing or regaining your internet connection.

**What is not recorded:** your questions or answers, keystrokes, clipboard contents, passwords, other
websites, other applications, or other devices.

**Who sees it:** your instructor, for review. These records are not proof of misconduct. An instructor
reviews them and may add notes. No penalty is applied automatically.

**Retention:** records are deleted automatically after [180] days (edit to match your school's policy).

**Questions or concerns:** contact your instructor: [instructor contact].
